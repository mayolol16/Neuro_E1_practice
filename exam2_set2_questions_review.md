### Question 1 (Part 1 (100 Qs) Original Q45) — UMN / LMN

**Stem:** Which statement correctly distinguishes upper from lower motor neurons?

- **A)** UMN axons exit the central nervous system to synapse on neuromuscular junctions 
- **B)** LMN cell bodies reside in the precentral gyrus and descend in the spinal cord 
- **C)** UMNs synapse on LMNs in the CNS, while LMNs exit the CNS to innervate muscle **(Correct)**
- **D)** Both classes of motor neurons extend axons into the peripheral nervous system 
- **E)** LMN cell bodies are confined strictly to the brainstem cranial nerve nuclei 

**Explanation:** UMN axons stay in the CNS and synapse on LMNs. LMN axons leave the CNS (cranial nerve or ventral root) to reach muscle.

*High-Yield Rule: UMNs synapse on LMNs in the CNS, while LMNs exit the CNS to innervate muscle*
