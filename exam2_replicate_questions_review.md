## Question 1 of 100
**Unit**: `Auditory & Vestibular` | **Blueprint Q#**: `Q43`

A passenger in an elevator feels the sensation of upward acceleration. Which receptor primarily detects this stimulus?

- **(A)** Macula of the saccule ✅
- **(B)** Macula of the utricle
- **(C)** Crista of the semicircular canal
- **(D)** Organ of Corti in the cochlea
- **(E)** Dorsal cochlear nucleus in the pons

**Correct Answer**: **(A)**

**Explanation**: The utricle senses horizontal linear acceleration and the saccule senses vertical linear acceleration. The semicircular canals sense rotation.

**High-Yield Pearl**: High-Yield Rule: Macula of the saccule
