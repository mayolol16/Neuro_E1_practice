### Question 1 (Original Q64) — Auditory & Vestibular System

**Stem:** A 52-year-old woman skips breakfast, stands in intense heat for one hour, feels sudden lightheadedness with tunnel vision, and experiences a transient syncopal episode. Neurologic examination is unremarkable. How is this dizziness classified?

- **A)** Peripheral vertigo from acute vestibular labyrinthitis 
- **B)** Vasovagal presyncope due to transient cerebral hypoperfusion **(Correct)**
- **C)** Central vertigo caused by posterior fossa brainstem ischemia 
- **D)** Benign paroxysmal positional vertigo from canalithiasis 
- **E)** Chronic persistent disequilibrium from cerebellar degeneration 

**Explanation:** Dizziness meaning lightheaded/faint is non-vestibular (hypoglycemia, dehydration, pooling), while vertigo means a sensation of spinning.

*High-Yield Rule: Vasovagal presyncope due to transient cerebral hypoperfusion*
