### Q1: [Visual System] Unit 1 Q1
A 32-year-old woman presents with 6 months of amenorrhea and milky nipple discharge. She reports frequently bumping into people and door frames on both sides. Visual field testing shows loss of the temporal field in both eyes. Which retinal fibers are most likely compressed?

- **A.** Uncrossed temporal retinal fibers from both eyes
- **B.** Crossing nasal retinal fibers from both eyes at the optic chiasm
- **C.** Both optic tracts
- **D.** Left optic nerve only
- **E.** Both lateral geniculate nuclei

<details><summary>Click to view Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>

**Correct Answer: B**

Nasal retinal fibers (temporal visual fields) cross at the optic chiasm. Compression from below by a pituitary adenoma (here a prolactinoma: amenorrhea, galactorrhea) interrupts these crossing fibers, producing bitemporal hemianopia ("tunnel vision"). Temporal retinal fibers remain uncrossed and are spared at the chiasm.

</details>

### Q6: [Visual System] Unit 1 Q6
In an experimental preparation, intracellular recordings are made from different retinal neurons during a light flash. Which cell type is the first in the visual pathway to generate action potentials, and how do photoreceptors respond to light?

- **A.** Ganglion cells; photoreceptors hyperpolarize in light
- **B.** Bipolar cells; photoreceptors hyperpolarize in light
- **C.** Ganglion cells; photoreceptors depolarize in light
- **D.** Photoreceptors; they depolarize in light
- **E.** Bipolar cells; photoreceptors depolarize in light

<details><summary>Click to view Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>

Photoreceptors are depolarized in the dark (releasing glutamate) and hyperpolarize in response to light; they do not generate action potentials. Bipolar cells also use graded potentials.
